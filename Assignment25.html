Short Note on Design Choices (Q25 Requirement)

Yeh note teacher ko dikhana compulsory hai -

  • Font Pairing: Playfair Display (Serif - headings ke liye elegant) + Poppins (Sans - body ke liye readable). Ye pairing professional lagti hai aur resume/portfolio me popular hai.
  • Color Palette: Primary #1A237E (Deep Navy - trust & knowledge), Accent #FFC107 (Amber - highlight ke liye), BG #F8F9FA (light grey - eye-friendly). High contrast, accessible.
  • Design Tokens (Q24): :root me CSS variables banaye --primary, --text-base etc. Taaki ek jagah se pura theme control ho sake. Maintainability ke liye best practice.
  • Light Styling: Heavy animations nahi, sirf rounded corners (12px), soft shadow, spacing. Clean & minimal rakha hai kyunki ye academic project hai.
  • Responsive: viewport meta tag + grid + max-width 1000px, mobile par bhi sahi dikhega.
  • Semantic + Accessibility: header, nav, main, section use kiya, har img me meaningful alt attribute diya.
